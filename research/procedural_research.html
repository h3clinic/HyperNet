Teach the CNN with generated shapes before its one real reference

The new contract: randomly initialize and train our own CNN using procedural images; then provide one real reference and its support box. No external pretrained weights and no natural-image pretraining. This is synthetic-only pretraining plus one real reference, not “no pretraining” or “one image total.”

The literature supports testing this direction. It does not demonstrate HyperNet’s exact task: recognizing other examples of a natural object category from one reference, without natural-image representation training. Most strong published results include substantial natural-data fine-tuning. The most directly relevant 2026 paper also shows that synthetic parameter similarity needs verification against rendered geometry.

A CPU experiment that preserves useful information

The following is our proposed engineering synthesis, not a reproduction or a claim that these papers endorse every choice. Start with a deterministic bank of geometric programs: connected segments, rings, holes, nested contours, radial harmonics and internal textures. Render multiple scales with antialiasing. Separate structural parameters from nuisance parameters so a background or color cannot reliably identify a synthetic family.

x = R(θ, ν)
θ: contour, connectivity, relative part arrangement
ν: placement, rotation, color, illumination, background

Gaussian variation should modify existing structure as well as pixels. Draw noise, smooth it spatially and use it as a bounded displacement field. A maximum displacement limit prevents large motions but alone cannot prevent folds; a bound on the field’s derivative is needed for that stronger claim. Check for empty masks, collapsed contours and destructive cropping. Gaussian noise is a sampling mechanism, not evidence of human reasoning or a probability model of category variation.

u = Gσ * ε;   T(p) = p + αu(p)
A sufficient contraction-style safeguard for injectivity is ||α∇u||op < 1 on the convex image domain.

Train a spatial feature map before global pooling. Preserve local gradients and fine patterns with a lightweight reconstruction or analytic-feature target, computed from the same transformed synthetic image. Asking the model to reconstruct an unwarped target after a warp would accidentally teach away the geometry. Use a separate small shape head for invariance and metric ordering so texture preservation and shape invariance do not issue contradictory commands to one output.

L = Ldetail + λpair ||zs(x₁) − zs(x₂)||²
    + λrank max(0, d(za,zp) − d(za,zn) + m)

For the paired term, retain geometry while independently changing nuisance. For ranking, use nearby perturbations within a compatible program family and verify their ordering with a rendered-mask distance. Include negatives that retain texture while changing contour or connectivity. Raw distances between unrelated parameter vectors are poor supervision: parameter units, phase wraparound and different program topologies can make those distances meaningless. A triplet margin discourages collapse, but still inspect embedding variance and held-out synthetic discrimination.

A practical initial pilot could use 256–1,024 base programs, batch32, 500–2,000 updates and 32–64px patches. These numbers are a bounded CPU experiment, not an optimum inferred from the papers. Count base programs, newly rendered images, augmented views and total exposures separately. Save the initialized seed, generator version and resulting checkpoint hash. Increase budget only after a controlled development comparison shows a reason.

Originality and remaining limits

Formula-driven pretraining, Gaussian deformations, metric triplets and analytic detail targets are established components. HyperNet’s possible contribution is their particular low-budget integration, its handling of one real reference, and an audit showing whether it improves category recognition. This review does not establish a new mathematical theorem, biological equivalence, priority or state-of-the-art performance.

Procedural shapes omit much of natural category variation: articulation, material changes, occlusion, context and views unseen in the support. More Gaussian samples do not supply those missing semantics automatically. A shared synthetic checkpoint also changes the training budget from earlier reference-only versions; comparisons must show that additional synthetic data explicitly. Any new improvement must survive fresh-photo evaluation before it is promoted.
