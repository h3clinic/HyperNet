Primary-source catalog

Find the reference inside a larger 2D image, while preserving the detail that identifies it.

This research follows the corrected2D goal. The proposed training data are flat procedural shapes, internal patterns and cluttered image scenes. There are no3D surfaces or camera-space normal targets. All CNN weights start randomly; the prior comes from our own synthetic training curriculum, not externally trained weights.

The relevant precedent is spatial Siamese matching

SiamFC compares convolutional feature maps of a small exemplar and larger search image by cross-correlation. This makes translation search efficient. Its random initialization is relevant to our constraint, but its published recipe uses2.5million sampled natural-video pairs. The2016 preprint was later revised in2021; the later timestamp is not a new2021 invention. We adopt the spatial-comparison idea, not the trained model or tracking benchmark claims. SiamFC primary paper.

CoAE separates finding plausible regions from deciding whether a region matches the support. It adds margin ranking to query-conditioned proposals. Its reduced ImageNet backbone still uses933,052 photos, followed by detection training and extra pretrained support filtering on COCO. These budgets prevent a fair numerical comparison with a synthetic-only pilot unless the complete protocol is rerun and disclosed. Its loss idea can still inform our implementation. CoAE primary paper.

Do not let a roughness shortcut masquerade as identity

A model can distinguish a smooth patch from a rough patch without recognizing which small pattern is present. The controlled negative should preserve the outline, broad color, pattern amplitude and frequency envelope, and change the micro-pattern identity. The positive preserves that identity while translation, scale, illumination and clutter vary. Both microstates must appear equally often as the reference, positive and negative; otherwise the network can learn a preferred pattern rather than compare two images.

s(r,q,b) = cosine(normalize(F(r)), normalize(ROI(F(q), b)))
L_micro_rank = max(0, margin + s(r,q_hard,b_hard) − s(r,q_positive,b_positive))
L_micro_match = BCE(logit(r,q,b), identity_matches)

These are proposed engineering losses, not a theorem or verbatim reproduction of a published architecture. Hold all-object localization supervision constant across arms. The family-only control may ignore micro-negative matching labels; the micro-aware arm adds those labels and ranking. That comparison tests the additional training objective under the same architecture, not architecture superiority. If boxes used in training are known renderer targets, evaluation must still use predicted proposals. Oracle-box matching belongs in a separately labeled diagnostic.

DenseCL argues for local correspondence objectives rather than relying only on a global image vector. Our procedural2D transforms can supply exact correspondence where visible, avoiding its feature-based match search. The source pretrains on COCO or ImageNet and fine-tunes on labeled downstream tasks, so its transfer scores are not evidence for one-source detection. DenseCL primary paper.

SuperPoint demonstrates that synthetic geometry can supply initial local-feature supervision. Its full system then adapts using80,000 COCO photographs; it is not a synthetic-only solution. Known planar transforms are useful supervision here, but matching a transformed local feature remains different from identifying every member of an unknown natural category. SuperPoint primary paper.

Visibility and compression need separate measurement

Keep a feature path before aggressive stride and spatial averaging. A2–6pixel pattern in the input may become subpixel after repeated downsampling; upsampling a coarse descriptor does not restore information that was lost. The shift-equivariance literature identifies aliasing as a real CNN failure mode. Filtering before subsampling can stabilize features, but removes unsupported frequencies. It is not a guarantee of minute-pattern preservation and does not justify blindly blurring the user's inputs. Zhang, ICML2019.

Audit the actual rendered difference between matched counterfactual scenes, support/query pattern size, and whether the changed pixels remain visible. Keep those measures as diagnostics. Do not remove difficult held-out examples after seeing model scores. A score improvement limited to large visible changes is useful, but must not be described as recognition of arbitrarily tiny details.

Thousands of compositions need a real holdout

A family identifier should encode the structural generating rule, not just a random seed. Splitting whole rule combinations gives a harder question than generating new parameters from the same rule. CoGenT illustrates holding out attribute combinations; we use that split-design principle only, not its3D images or visual-question-answering task. CLEVR/CoGenT official description.

The planned2,048 training compositions and128 DEV plus128 TEST compositions should have disjoint canonical grammar keys. Reused operators are expected and must be disclosed. An unseen combination of familiar operators is compositional transfer; it is not an unseen natural semantic category. If some random combinations are visually equivalent, counts of distinct program keys can overstate perceptual diversity. Preserve examples and factor-frequency tables so that limitation is inspectable.

Does it find any plausible object?Proposal recall at IoU≥0.5, before support matching.

Does acceptance point to the correct location?Joint accepted-and-IoU≥0.5 recall, with all-positive localization reported.
Does the added objective distinguish small patterns?Counterbalanced paired-positive/negative accuracy, margins and failures stratified by actual visible change.

Cluster uncertainty by held-out family because paired scenes, references and microstates are dependent. Freeze the generator, steps, checkpoints and objective coefficients before TEST. The resulting contribution is an auditable2D synthetic detection experiment. Neither a large family count nor an improved synthetic score establishes human-like reasoning, a new mathematical primitive, or improved natural-photo generalization.
